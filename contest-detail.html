23rd All India HOPE Talent Contest (Art & Essay)

Dear Participants,

Greetings from SchoolsIndia!

SchoolsIndia, an ISO 9001:2015 certified company , has proudly conducted the All India HOPE Talent Contest in Art and Essay Writing for over 20 years, reaching schools across the nation.

We are thrilled to announce the 23rd edition of this prestigious contest - with more exciting awards, rewards, and cash prizes than ever before!

We warmly invite students from all affiliated schools to participate and showcase their creativity. This contest uncovers and recognizes hidden talents, bringing recognition to both the students and their schools.

Contest Specifications

Entry Sheet :  Provided by SchoolsIndia after fee submission at the school.

Essay :

  • Must be handwritten, clear, and legible.
  • Should not exceed 500 words. 

Drawing : 

  • Use Watercolour, pencil colour, oil paint, sketch pen, or crayon.
  • Allowed on separate sheet, canvas, or any material (Max size: 10x15 inches)

Terms & Conditions

  • Participation is strictly through schools; no direct entries will be accepted. 
  • Entry sheets will be provided by SchoolsIndia after fee submission at the school
  • Entry sheets will be provided by SchoolsIndia after fee submission at the school
  • Contest can be conducted within the school premises.
  • The Head of the Institution or Teacher-in-Charge must certify entries as original.
  • Submitted materials become the property of SchoolsIndia.
  • The decision of the judges will be final and binding.

Awards & Rewards

  • Certificate of Appreciation for all participants. (Students participating in both Art and Essay will receive  two certificates.)
  • Merit Certificates & Medals  for best entries from each school.
  • Cash Prizes  for over 1500 students at the National Level.
  • Winners’ names with class & school will be displayed on www.schoolsindia.com
  • Top Art Entries may be selected for the  Yearly Calendar or Contest Poster.
  • Opportunity to gain   national recognitionon a unique platform.
  • Total    Cash Prizes worth over Rs. 5,00,000/-will be distributed.
