Online Admission

The Online Admission Software Services is with updated feature from Schoolsindia, which is a great boon for admission seekers in allied categories namely CBSE, ICSE and all State Board levels in India.

This product is designed keeping in mind today's growing IT solutions through the internet and in helping the Schools and Parents a hassle-free service who face many difficulties during admission time.

The schools are more benefitted by using the Online Admission Software as it saves their precious time, resources and administrative costs because it fully automates the work done manually by the school staff. The Government of India is also encouraging schools for going online, under Digital India Policy.

Some of the salient features of the software are

  • 01. Online Admission is compatible with Computer, Laptop or Mobile phone, for Schools & Parents.
  • 02. School can customize their admission form with their own fields and criteria.
  • 03. Admission notification link can be placed anywhere in the school website.
  • 04. Admission starting date & time and closing date & time can be pre-set.
  • 05. School can set their own age criteria, so it will only accept forms for age between particular dates.
  • 06. School can also set the maximum no of application to be issued online, or can restrict the applications. (Duplicate application can also be blocked, if required by school).
  • 07. Each applicant (parent) will receive unique auto-generated (set by school) application number both by Email and SMS after online submission.
  • 08. After the school's approval only the registration forms will be send to parent's email id for interview.
  • 09. School can view the reports as to how many applicants applied, how many having sibling, alumni, quota, residence distance from school etc.
  • 10. School can also accept online payment for their admission forms directly, if they wish.
  • 11. School already having website / online admission, our Online Admission features can be applied easily.

Our Online Admission Software Services has been made so simple, which can be managed by any staff with basic computer knowledge and little bit of training. In order to ensure trouble-free usage of the software, we also provide a help file to the schools to manage their admission process and instructions to the parents to fill the forms.
